SITE INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

This portfolio uses basic analytics and visitor-security monitoring to understand website usage, improve reliability, and protect the site.

Information that may be collected

  • A random visitor identifier stored in this browser and a session identifier.
  • Public IP address and network metadata made available to the hosting platform, such as approximate country/city, network organisation and ASN where available.
  • Browser, operating system, device category, language, screen dimensions and connection hints provided by the browser.
  • Pages visited, referring website, campaign parameters, approximate engagement events, performance measurements and technical errors.

How the information is used

Information is used for aggregate website analytics, returning-visit recognition in the same browser, performance diagnostics, abuse detection and operational alerts to the site owner. Visitor records are stored for up to 365 days and then expire automatically, subject to platform limits and backup/operational constraints.

Limitations

A visitor ID identifies a browser profile, not a verified person. Clearing browser storage or switching browsers/devices may create a different ID. Location, carrier, network type, device model, VPN and proxy signals may be unavailable or approximate. No attempt is made to identify a person across unrelated devices.

What is not collected

This tracker is not designed to collect passwords, clipboard contents, keystrokes, contact-form message contents, payment details or uploaded files. Avoid entering sensitive information into public forms.

Choices

You can clear this website's local storage/cookies in your browser to reset its visitor identifier. Some basic server-side security information may still be processed when requests reach the hosting platform.
